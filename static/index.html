Stability fingerprints

Capture the selected segment at the current boundary displacement. Compare its boundary response with another organ, another mask, or a later edit.

Profiles are stored in this browser on this site. Export JSON to back them up or move between browsers. No CT voxels are stored in profiles.

How to read this fingerprint

Curves show percentage volume change relative to each mask’s own baseline. Heatmap cells show loss or gain at fixed offsets, using one shared color scale. Regional bars show the fraction of baseline volume in 10 mm regions with each erosion-loss range at −2 mm; they are weighted by voxel count, not region count. Difference = B minus A in percentage points. No composite stability score or accuracy claim is made.

Grid placement, voxel spacing, organ size, and scan boundaries affect these results. Low displacement may produce no voxel changes. Same-scan checks compare image content and geometry; they do not verify that the segments describe the same organ. Profiles contain labels and derived measurements; use non-identifying names.

INTERPRETATION

What does this show?

Even small boundary shifts may change voxel-based volume estimates. This tool is an artificial perturbation experiment, not a measurement of real algorithm errors.
